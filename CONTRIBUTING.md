# Contributing to Picantex

Thank you for your interest!

- **Bug reports and ideas are welcome.** Open an issue here, or a ticket at https://picantex.app/ticket/ if you
  prefer not to use GitHub. Please describe what you did, what you expected and what happened, and include your
  browser and its version.
- **Code contributions only by prior agreement.** Please open an issue to discuss a change before writing code, and
  do not open pull requests without an agreement. The author also publishes other editions of Picantex, so any
  contributed code can only be accepted together with a written agreement that allows it to be used in them.
- **Security or privacy problems:** please report them privately through a ticket at https://picantex.app/ticket/
  rather than in a public issue.
